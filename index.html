Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

DAILYGO (“DAILYGO,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) is a personal planning and money-management utility operated by Epaynex Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the DAILYGO mobile application and related services.

Information we handle

Depending on the features you use, DAILYGO may handle:

  • Account and profile information: email address, display name, country, preferred currency, and authentication identifiers when you create or use an account.
  • Information you enter: tasks, reminders, goals, income and expense entries, budget categories and amounts, calculator inputs, saved tools, and calculation history. Entries may contain personal information if you choose to include it.
  • Service and device information: app version, device and operating-system details, diagnostic events, and technical logs needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the service. App or hosting infrastructure may process IP address and request metadata.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting us.

The app includes account-based planning, goals, manually entered money tracking, calculators, saved tools, and history. The codebase also contains an AI assistant feature; when that feature is enabled and used, the additional AI processing described below applies. Features may vary by release.

How information is used

  • Create and authenticate accounts and maintain sessions.
  • Provide and save the planning, goal, budgeting, money-tracking, calculator, and history features you request.
  • Display your entries and calculate summaries on your behalf.
  • Maintain, protect, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate essential service or policy updates.
  • Comply with applicable law and enforce our terms.

We do not use the contents of personal task, money, or calculator entries for advertising. We do not sell personal information.

Product analytics

DAILYGO records limited product-activity events in its Supabase database, linked to your account identifier. Events may describe actions such as creating or completing a task or goal, recording or updating income or expenses, using a calculator or saved tool, or using the currency converter. Event properties may include limited context such as a category, priority, calculator/tool identifier, whether a note was present, or whether a live rate was used. These event records are used to understand feature use and improve the service; event properties are designed not to include task titles, expense descriptions, or entered amounts.

AI assistant processing

If the AI assistant is enabled and you use it, your prompts and the relevant conversation messages are sent to the configured AI model provider to generate a response. The default provider endpoint in the app code is OpenAI; the configured provider may differ. Conversation titles, messages, and responses are stored in our Supabase database and associated with your account so you can view recent conversations. Avoid entering passwords or highly sensitive information into AI conversations.

Storage and sharing

Account and user-created records are processed by our application infrastructure and Supabase-hosted database and authentication services. Technical data may also be processed by hosting, security, and diagnostic providers that help operate the service.

We may disclose limited information to service providers acting on our instructions; when required by law or legal process; to protect users, the service, or our rights; or in connection with a business transfer subject to appropriate protections. Providers may process information only to deliver services to us. We do not sell or rent personal information to data brokers or advertisers.

Security and retention

We use access controls and other reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

We retain account and user-created information while needed to provide the service and for legitimate operational, security, or legal purposes. Technical logs may be retained for shorter periods according to operational needs. Retention periods vary by information type and applicable obligations.

Your choices and account deletion

You may review or change information through available app settings and stop using DAILYGO at any time.

The app currently does not provide an in-app account-deletion control. To request deletion of your account and associated personal information, email mydailygo@gmail.com from your registered email address or provide enough information for us to verify your account. We will verify the request and delete or anonymize associated personal information unless retention is required or permitted by law. Residual copies may remain in backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

Children

DAILYGO is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

International processing

Service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as DAILYGO changes or legal requirements evolve. We will update the effective date and provide notice in the app or by other appropriate means when material changes are made.
