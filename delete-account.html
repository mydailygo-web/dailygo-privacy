Delete your DAILYGO account

Epaynex Inc. accepts requests to delete DAILYGO accounts and associated personal data. You do not need to sign in to make a request.

To request deletion: email mydailygo@gmail.com from the email address registered to your DAILYGO account. Include “DAILYGO account deletion request” in the subject. If you cannot access your registered email, explain this so support can arrange another way to verify account ownership.

Do not send your password or financial records in the request.

What happens after a request

Support must verify account ownership before processing the request. Opening or sending an email does not itself delete your account or confirm that the request was received. Support must process the deletion and confirm completion. No fixed processing time is promised.

Data covered

A request covers the account login and profile, preferences, tasks and reminders, goals and progress, income and expenses, budgets, saved tools, calculation history, and any account-linked records from features previously used, including conversations, referrals, rewards, and product activity.

Retention exceptions

Some records may need to be retained where required or permitted by law. Residual copies may remain in backups for a period under the applicable backup-retention process. Support will explain any applicable exception. No fixed backup-expiry period is promised.
